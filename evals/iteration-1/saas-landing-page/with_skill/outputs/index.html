Questions agency owners ask

Will my clients know the meeting is being recorded?

Minutely joins as a visible participant, so everyone on the call can see it. We recommend telling clients up front, and you can add a line about it to your meeting invites.

What happens to our client conversations?

Your meeting content belongs to you. Notes are only visible to the people you share them with, and you can delete any meeting and its recording at any time.

Do I need to change the tools we use?

No. Minutely works alongside your calendar and video calls, and you can copy action items into whatever project tool your team prefers.

How long does setup take?

Connecting your calendar takes a couple of minutes. Your first notes arrive after your next meeting.

What happens when the free trial ends?

You choose whether to continue. We won't ask for a card to start, so you won't be charged by surprise.
