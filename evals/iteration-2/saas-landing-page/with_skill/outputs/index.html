Every client call creates an hour of admin nobody bills for.

Someone has to write the recap, chase the decisions, and remember who promised what. In a small agency, that someone is usually you or your best account manager.

When it slips, the client notices. A missed follow-up looks like a missed deadline, and the thread of who said what lives only in one person's head.

Minutely takes that job off your team's plate and keeps a clean, searchable record of every client conversation.

Do my clients need to know it is there?

Minutely shows as a named participant in the call, and you can add a line to your invite. Most agencies tell clients up front that notes are taken automatically. Check the recording rules where you and your clients are based.

How accurate are the notes?

Good enough to send after a quick read, not good enough to send blind. That is why every recap and task list goes to you for review first, and why unclear owners are flagged instead of guessed.

What happens to our meeting data?

Your recordings and notes belong to you. You can delete any meeting at any time, and you can choose to keep audio or transcripts only for a set period.

Which tools does it work with?

Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, plus Google and Outlook calendars. Tasks can be copied out as a list or sent to your project tool.

What does the free trial include?

Everything. You can try it on real client meetings with your whole team, and you do not need a credit card to begin.
