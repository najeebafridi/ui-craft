Tern

A hand-thrown ceramic pour-over kettle. Made slowly, to pour slowly.

Scroll

01 · The clay

One lump of clay, thrown on the wheel in about four minutes.

Every Tern starts as a ball on a spinning wheel. No moulds, so no two kettles share quite the same curve.

02 · The spout

A long, thin neck for a slow, steady stream.

Pour-over coffee needs a thread of water, not a gush. The spout is pulled by hand and tested on every kettle before it is glazed.

03 · The glaze

Three glazes, fired to 1,240 °C.

Chalk: soft, matt, like a pebble from the shore.
