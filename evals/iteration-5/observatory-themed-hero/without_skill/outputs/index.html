What you'll see this month

Late autumn is the start of the best season. The nights are long and the Milky Way still stretches overhead before the winter stars climb.

Leonids, peak 17–18 Nov

Fast, bright streaks from comet Tempel–Tuttle. Expect around 10 to 15 an hour from a dark site, and the occasional fireball that lights up the forest.

Best between 23:00 and 05:00. No kit needed.

Getting here

We are on a hilltop with no street lights and limited mobile signal. Plan the route before you leave home.

Kielder Dark Sky Observatory Black Fell, Kielder Water & Forest Park Northumberland NE48 1XX

Arrive 15 minutes early. The last mile is a forest track.

Use a red torch. White light ruins everyone's night vision. We lend them at the gate.

Dress for 5 degrees colder than the forecast, with a hat and gloves.

By car

55 min from Hexham, 1 h 20 from Newcastle. Follow signs for Kielder Castle, then the observatory. Free parking for 60 cars.

By bus

No evening public service reaches the site. Taxis from Bellingham take about 35 minutes: book your return in advance, as nothing is available on the night.

Accessibility

Level path from the car park, a step-free viewing deck and a heated room with seating. Tell us about your needs when booking and we will plan around them.

Stay nearby

Kielder Village has a pub and two guesthouses, five minutes' drive away. Book early on weekends.
