Dates and booking

Each night runs about three hours, outdoors, whatever the temperature. Pick a night, say how many are coming, and we hold your places.

Choose a night

Moon lit is the share of the Moon's face that is bright at 20:00. A bright Moon washes out faint stars, so those nights focus on the Moon and the planets. Dark nights show the Milky Way.

What you'll see this month

Your eyes need about twenty minutes in the dark to take in a sky like this. On the hill we give you the full twenty before anyone picks up a telescope.

  • The Milky Way

    Naked eyeBest 4 to 12 Dec, after dusk

    The faint arch of our own galaxy, with its dark dust lane, rises from the forest edge when the Moon is out of the way.

  • Leonid meteors

    Naked eyePeak 17 to 18 Nov, tail on 20 Nov

    Fast, faint streaks from comet dust. A few an hour on our first night; lie back on a mat, we lend them.

  • Andromeda Galaxy

    Naked eyeBinocularsHigh in the evening

    A smudge of light 2.5 million years old, and the furthest thing most people will ever see without a telescope.

  • Saturn

    TelescopeSouth after dusk, all month

    The rings and one or two moons, steady enough to hold a queue of small gasps.

  • The Moon up close

    Telescope20, 21 and 27 Nov

    Craters and mountain shadows along the edge of the lit side. A bright Moon is the best show we have when the faint stars are drowned out.

  • Pleiades and Orion

    Naked eyeTelescopePleiades early, Orion by 22:00

    A young star cluster in the east, then the Orion Nebula, a star nursery glowing in the sword.

Getting there

The last few miles are a forest road with no street lights. That is the point, and it is why a little planning helps.

Kielder Dark Sky ObservatoryKielder Forest, NorthumberlandPostcode and what3words are in your booking email.
  • By carFree car park on the hill. Follow the observatory signs from Kielder village, dipped headlights only for the last mile.
  • By public transportNo buses run after dark. A taxi from Bellingham takes about 25 minutes; book it before you come.
  • ArriveBetween 18:45 and 19:15 for a 19:30 start. Doors are closed once the night begins so no white light spoils anyone's eyes.
  • Phone signalPatchy to none. Save your booking email before you leave.
  • AccessA step-free path from the car park to the main deck. Tell us in advance if you need help and we will meet you.
Wear more than you thinkIt is several degrees colder than the valley, and you will stand still. Layers, a hat, gloves, sturdy shoes. We lend red torches; white ones stay in the car.
