Made in Stoke-on-Trent

Tern is thrown on the wheel, dried, fired, glazed and fired again, a few at a time, in a small pottery in the city that has made ceramics for centuries.

Because each kettle is made by hand, glaze and shape vary a little from one to the next. That is the point of buying one.
