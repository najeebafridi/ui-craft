Questions owners ask first

Do my clients know a bot is on the call?

Yes. Minutely joins as a named participant and posts a short note in the chat. You can turn that note off for internal calls only.

How accurate are the notes?

Good enough to send after a quick read, not good enough to send blind. That is why every recap waits for your approval, and you can edit any line.

Where does our meeting data go?

It’s stored encrypted, kept per client, and never used to train models. Recordings are deleted once the notes exist, unless you ask us to keep them.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing, unless you add a card. Your notes stay readable and exportable. Upgrade whenever you’re ready.

Does it work for calls in other languages?

Notes can be written in the language of the call or in English. Check the list of supported languages inside the app during your trial.
