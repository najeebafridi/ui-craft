Questions agency owners ask

How accurate are the notes?

Minutely handles accents, crosstalk and fast conversation well, and you can edit any line before you share it. Adding client names and jargon to your word list improves results further.

Do my clients need to install anything?

No. Minutely joins as a guest on your meeting link, and it is named in the participant list so everyone knows it is there.

What happens when the trial ends?

You choose. Add a card to keep going, or do nothing and your account pauses. Your notes stay available to export for 30 days. We never charge you without asking first.

Can I keep some meetings private?

Yes. Minutely only joins the meetings you select. Skip it for a call with one click, or turn it off for a whole client.

Does it work for in-person meetings?

Yes. Open Minutely on your phone or laptop, press record in the room, and get the same notes and action items afterwards.
