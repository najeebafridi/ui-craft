## Teardowns

### Productivity / dev
- **Linear.** Public redesign post: theme rebuilt in LCH colour so brightness looks even across hues. A theme needs three inputs (base, accent, contrast) instead of 98 variables. Brand blue was toned down in the neutrals for a calmer look. Inter Display for headings, Inter for body (Inter is OFL, free). Navigation, tabs and headers were tightened to cut noise and follow Apple-style native conventions. Stated goal: cohesive and timeless, not a flashy overhaul. Dark-first, near-monochrome, one violet-blue accent, thin low-contrast borders, minimal shadow (unverified for the marketing site). Signature: extreme restraint and speed feel; the chrome disappears so the issue list is the hero.
- **Vercel.** Black/white with hairline borders, a mono-leaning geometric grotesk (Geist, OFL, free; the company publishes it). Very sharp, almost square radius, little colour, color appears in status and gradients used sparingly. Docs and marketing are dense with code (unverified). Signature: stark monochrome plus a grid of hairlines and code as imagery.
- **Raycast.** Dark, glossy, keyboard-first. Key-cap graphics, soft red/orange accent, fine inner borders and glow on dark surfaces (unverified). Copy is short and verb-led. Signature: UI shown as keyboard shortcuts and the command bar.
- **Notion.** Warm paper-white neutrals, near-black text, slightly warm greys, hand-drawn black-line illustration with a few flat colours on marketing. Serif option inside the product (unverified). Copy is calm and plain. Signature: friendly, doodle-style illustrations beside a very quiet editor.
- **Arc (The Browser Company).** Playful and colour-forward for a productivity tool: saturated gradients as personal themes, rounded forms, a witty, personal voice (unverified). Signature: user-chosen colour as identity, with soft glass sidebar.
- **Superhuman.** Dark, dense, keyboard-only product; marketing leans on large editorial headlines and tight copy about speed (unverified). Signature: performance as the brand, shortcuts everywhere.
- **Things (Cultured Code).** Native Apple look: white and light-grey, tiny blue accent, soft rounded checkboxes, generous rows, subtle animation that reflects the physical act of checking something off (unverified). Won Apple Design Awards in the past. Signature: calm focus and a finish that feels like a physical object.
- **Figma.** Neutral, grey chrome that gets out of the way of the canvas. Marketing is much louder: bright multi-colour blocks, big bold type, collage (unverified). Signature: a neutral tool shell with a colourful brand outside it.

- **Wise.** Fresh bright-green accent with dark forest green, big friendly sans (a custom face), hero numbers for exchange rates, transparent-fee tone. The public wise.design page gave little detail, so palette and type are (unverified). Signature: radical transparency in copy and a confident green.
- **Revolut.** Dark-mode-first, black with light type, large product imagery, wide-set headline sans (a custom face, unverified), a young, bold voice. Signature: card-on-black imagery and large numerals.
- **Monzo.** Hot coral on warm cream/navy, rounded humane type, friendly, jargon-free tone of voice, lively illustration (unverified; search did not return its guidelines). Signature: tone of voice as brand.

### Consumer / lifestyle / commerce
- **Airbnb.** Own typeface Cereal (introduced 2018 to unify the brand across media; described as friendly, rounded, polished). White canvas, big photographs, a warm coral-red accent, generous radius, light shadows. Signature: photography first, search bar as the hero.
- **Apple (product pages).** System-adjacent grotesque (SF Pro; licensed for Apple platforms only), huge headlines, alternating full-bleed light and black panels, product photography with soft studio light, spacing that is very generous, scroll-driven reveals (unverified for current details). Signature: one idea per screen and product-as-hero.
- **Aesop.** Warm cream and umber, small serif plus a neutral sans in a restrained editorial layout, product bottles photographed in amber on neutral ground, long literary copy (unverified). Signature: apothecary tone, text-led and unhurried.
- **Headspace / Calm.** Headspace: flat, round-bodied illustration characters, saturated orange on warm, soft pastels. Calm: nature photography and gentle blue gradients, slow motion (both unverified). Signature: soft shapes that lower the heart rate.
- **Duolingo.** Custom headline face Feather Bold (Fontsmith for Johnson Banks, 2019, inspired by the owl mascot) and DIN Next Rounded for body (per search summaries). - **Spotify.** Circular (Lineto, commercial) from the 2015 rebrand, later a custom variant (Spotify Mix, Dinamo). Dark default, bright green accent, duotone images with strong colour, album art as the colour source. Signature: dark canvas where cover art provides all the colour.
- **Nike.** Photography of athletes, full-bleed black and white, condensed bold uppercase headlines (Futura-derived condensed, unverified), minimal UI chrome, rapid motion. Signature: monumental type over action imagery.
- **Luxury house (Hermes / Rolex style, public pages).** Large uncluttered photography, thin serif or light sans in small sizes with wide tracking, huge whitespace, muted palette (orange or green only as brand accent, unverified), almost no promotional UI, slow transitions. ### Health / gov / education

- **Khan Academy.** Friendly teal/green brand, open-source Wonder Blocks design system (public, licence unverified), warm illustration, large readable type, encouraging copy (unverified). Signature: approachable and learner-first, content before chrome.

## Standards of excellence

- **Awwwards** (official evaluation page via search). Four weighted criteria: Design 40%, Usability 30%, Creativity 20%, Content 10%. At least 18 jurors score each approved site on a 10-point scale; the three scores furthest from the average are dropped; Site of the Day needs about 8.0+. Older write-ups mention mobile and developer scores, so treat as secondary. Take-away: craft and usability outweigh novelty; creativity is only a fifth.
- **Apple Design Awards.** Six categories in 2025 and 2026: Delight and Fun, Inclusivity, Innovation, Interaction, Social Impact, Visuals and Graphics; one app and one game winner each. Older criteria were summarised as design, technology and innovation. Winners are judged on craft and ingenuity, not downloads. Per-category criteria text was not retrieved (unverified).
- **Webby Awards.** Judging criteria: content, structure and navigation, visual design, functionality, interactivity, innovation, overall experience (per search summary of the official page).
- **Google Play / Material awards.** Not retrieved. Treat as unverified: Play Best-of awards emphasise quality, adaptive layouts and accessibility. Material design guidance stresses adaptive layout, motion with purpose, accessibility.
- **GOV.UK / accessibility as a standard.** WCAG 2.2 AA is the practical floor for premium (see §6/9 of the base skill).
- **Luxury vs premium tech vs friendly consumer** (blog sources on luxury only; the tech-vs-luxury comparison itself is my synthesis):
  - Luxury: large negative space as a confidence signal, minimal navigation, editorial layouts, photography given room, bespoke or minimal type, restrained palette, slow motion, few promotions.
  - Premium tech: precision. Tight grid, hairline borders, neutral palette with a single accent, fast and crisp motion, code or product UI as imagery, density that signals expertise.
  - Friendly consumer: warmth. Rounded type and shapes, saturated colour, mascots or illustration, conversational copy, large touch targets, celebratory motion.

## AI design skills (2026 check)

Star counts and versions come from third-party aggregators or the repo page and vary, so treat as approximate.
- **anthropics/skills frontend-design** (licence per catalog: Apache-2.0 for skills in that repo, LICENSE.txt in folder, unverified). Raw SKILL.md read. Steps: identify subject, audience and primary job; draw visual choices from the subject's own world; write a compact plan (palette, type, layout, principles); check it against the brief for generic defaults and revise; build; critique with screenshots; cut what doesn't serve. One element carries the boldness, the rest stays restrained. Anti-slop list is specific: cream plus terracotta, near-black plus acid green, broadsheet hairlines, identical rounded SaaS cards with gradient washes, all-caps eyebrows, monospace data labels, italicising one headline word, arrows on links, numbering without sequence, motion everywhere. Copy rules: sentence case, verbs, errors that explain and do not apologise. Idea to borrow: the plan is checked against its own default.
- **pbakaus/impeccable** (Apache-2.0; v3.1.1 per aggregator, May 2026; about 24 commands). Builds on frontend-design. Commands to steer (bolder, quieter, distil, colour, type, layout, motion), review (critique scored, audit technical), finish (polish, harden, adapt, clarify copy), live (pick an element, cycle variants), plan/setup. Context files PRODUCT.md (audience, constraints) and DESIGN.md (visual system) keep output on-brand. 62 deterministic anti-pattern rules need no LLM. Idea: durable context files plus a detector.
- **Leonxlnx/taste-skill** (MIT; ~116k stars). Classify the business type first, plan screen by screen, one design system, one section per screen, real photos and product UI rather than filler, shader/scroll effects only for tech, calmer type and photo-led motion elsewhere; headless-browser checker and a finish gate (empty visuals, contrast, misalignment, placeholder text). ## Synthesis: visual directions

Names are my own. Fonts listed are free (OFL or similar) unless noted. Neutral temperature: cool / neutral / warm. Chroma is low (0-3%), mid, high.

1. **Quiet instrument** (dark or light, dev tools, B2B workflow, power-user SaaS; professionals who live in it all day). Feeling: calm, fast, in control. Neutrals cool, very low chroma, 10 steps; accent one violet/blue at mid saturation, under 5% of surface. Dark default option. Type: neutral grotesque (Inter, Geist). Radius 4-6. Hairline borders, almost no shadow. Density compact. Motion: instant 100-150ms, no flourish. Imagery: real product UI. Voice: terse, verbs.
2. **Paper and ink** (writing, notes, knowledge tools, publishing, docs; thoughtful adults). Feeling: calm, literate. Neutrals warm off-white, near-black text; accent small, ink-blue or brick. Light. Type: serif for reading (Source Serif 4, Newsreader) plus plain sans (Inter). Radius 4-8. Borders minimal, no shadow. Density standard, readable 65-75ch. Motion: almost none. Imagery: line illustration or none. Voice: plain, gently dry.
3. **Studio gloss** (consumer pro hardware, camera, audio, premium apps; people who want to be impressed). Feeling: expensive, polished. Neutrals neutral black and white, large panels alternate; accent minimal, product colours carry. Type: system-style grotesque (Inter, Geist), very large headlines. Radius 12-24 on media. Soft layered shadows only on product. Spacious. Motion: scroll-driven reveals, one idea per screen. Imagery: studio product photos. Voice: short, declarative.
4. **Maison** (luxury goods, hotels, jewellery, fine dining, high fashion; wealthy or aspirational buyers). Feeling: quiet confidence, desire. Neutrals warm, stone/ivory/charcoal; accent either none or one deep brand tone (oxblood, bottle green), low saturation. Light or deep dark. Type: high-contrast display serif at modest size (Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Fraunces) plus light sans; tracked small caps for nav. Radius 0-2. No shadows, hairlines. Very spacious (96-160px sections). Motion: slow fades 400-600ms (a rare exception to the 400ms rule; keep it to page-level). Imagery: large, cropped, few. Voice: sparse, third person, no exclamation.
5. **Apothecary editorial** (skincare, wellness retail, craft food, boutique; considered shoppers). Feeling: warm, intelligent, unhurried. Neutrals warm, amber/cream/umber; accent earthy and muted (olive, rust used once). Type: small serif or humanist sans (Libre Caslon, EB Garamond with Inter). Radius 0-4. Flat, thin rules. Spacious but text-rich. Motion: subtle. Imagery: product on neutral ground, natural light. Voice: literary, specific. Caution: this is the cream-and-terracotta cliché if applied without real photography.
6. **Soft wellness** (meditation, sleep, therapy, health, family; anxious or stressed users). Feeling: safe, slow. Neutrals warm pastel tints; accent calming mid-saturation blue, sage or peach. Light, with a dark night mode. Type: rounded humanist sans (Nunito, Quicksand, DM Sans). Radius 16-28. Very soft diffuse shadows. Spacious, large targets. Motion slow ease, breathing. Imagery: soft illustration or nature photos. Voice: kind, second person, no pressure.
7. **Playful game-feel** (learning, kids, habits, fitness, consumer games; broad, young audiences). Feeling: fun, rewarding. Neutrals neutral light; accent saturated, 2-3 bright hues with dark outlines. Type: rounded bold display (Nunito Black, Baloo 2, Fredoka). Radius 12-20. Chunky borders and a solid bottom-edge "lip" instead of blur shadows. Standard density, big targets. Motion: springy with bounce on reward, tied to actions. Imagery: mascot/illustration. Voice: cheeky, encouraging, short.
8. **Trusted ledger** (banking, insurance, payroll, invoices, accounting; money-sensitive adults). Feeling: safe and competent but modern. Neutrals cool light grey or white; accent deep blue, teal or green, medium-dark for AA. Light. Type: neutral sans (Inter, IBM Plex Sans) with tabular numerals. Radius 6-8. Fine borders, restrained shadow. Compact to standard. Motion functional. Imagery: product screens, simple charts. Voice: plain, exact, fee-and-number transparency.
9. **Neo-bank bold** (consumer finance, cards, crypto-adjacent apps, subscriptions; younger adults). Feeling: confident, current. Neutrals true black or off-white; accent one vivid signature (lime, coral, electric blue). Often dark-first. Type: wide or geometric grotesque (Space Grotesk, Sora, Manrope). Radius 12-24 or pill on buttons. Mostly flat colour blocks. Standard density, huge numerals. Motion quick, card flips. Imagery: card/device mockups. Voice: casual, direct, human.
10. **Public clarity** (government, health portals, utilities, legal forms, education services; everyone including low literacy and assistive tech). Feeling: dependable, fair. Neutrals white and black, low chroma greys; accent strong link blue and a thick high-contrast focus colour (yellow). Light. Type: plain sans (Public Sans, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Source Sans 3), 18-19px body. Radius 0-4. Strong borders, zero shadow. Spacious in text, single column. Motion: none. Imagery: almost none. Voice: plain language, one question per page.
11. **Open-air marketplace** (travel, rentals, food delivery, classifieds; mass market). Feeling: inviting, trustworthy. Neutrals white with warm grey; accent warm coral/red, one only. Light. Type: friendly geometric sans (Nunito Sans, DM Sans, Figtree). Radius 12-16. Light shadows on lifted cards, photo-led. Standard density, search as the hero. Motion: gentle hover, image carousels. Imagery: real people and places, big. Voice: warm, welcoming.
12. **Stage dark** (media, music, video, gaming, events; entertainment fans). Feeling: immersive. Neutrals near-black with slightly tinted surfaces (not pure black), raised surfaces lighter; accent bright green, magenta or orange pulled from content. Dark default. Type: confident geometric sans (Outfit, Manrope) or condensed display for headlines (Barlow Condensed, Oswald). Radius 4-12. Depth from surface lightness, not shadow. Standard-compact. Motion: cinematic, parallax only where it serves content. Imagery: artwork/posters supply colour, duotone treatments. Voice: short, hype-light.
13. **Athletic monument** (sportswear, fitness brands, campaigns, product drops; performance buyers). Feeling: powerful, urgent. Neutrals black/white, hard contrast; accent used as a single colour block. Type: heavy condensed uppercase display (Anton, Barlow Condensed, Bebas Neue) over a plain sans. Radius 0. No shadows. Spacious with giant type. Motion: fast cuts, large scale shifts. Imagery: full-bleed action photography. Voice: imperative, 2-4 words.
14. **Graph-paper technical** (APIs, infrastructure, security, data and AI platforms, docs; engineers). Feeling: rigorous, credible. Neutrals cool white or black, visible grid and hairlines; accent gradient-free single hue plus status colours. Both modes. Type: grotesque plus mono (Geist, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono). Radius 2-6. Hairline, dashed grid. Dense. Motion: purposeful diagrams drawing in. Imagery: code, diagrams, terminal. Voice: exact, no marketing adjectives.
15. **Bright workshop** (design, creative, collaboration tools marketing; teams and creators). Feeling: energetic but organised. Neutrals neutral shell; accent multi-hue bright blocks outside the tool. Light. Type: bold grotesque (Plus Jakarta Sans, Bricolage Grotesque, Space Grotesk). Radius 8-20. Flat colour fields, offset collage. Standard density. Motion: playful, scroll-triggered, one signature. Imagery: collage, illustrated people. Voice: witty, human. (Directions 1-15; the brief asked for 10-14, so merge 15 into 3 or 11 if space is tight.)

## Common denominators of premium

1. A decided system: few sizes, one spacing scale, one radius, one accent. Nothing is incidental.
2. Hierarchy by restraint: one hero per screen, everything else quieter.
3. Typography does the heavy lifting: a considered face, large confident headlines, tight line height at display size, good rhythm in text.
4. Generous or deliberately dense space, never in-between mush: luxury spacious, tools compact, but alignment is exact.
5. A neutral base tinted toward the brand with a single accent that is used rarely, and contrast that actually passes.
6. Real content and imagery: actual product UI, photography with a consistent grade, or a unique illustration language; no stock filler.
7. Depth with discipline: hairlines or very soft shadows, never decorative glow.
8. Motion that explains: short, eased, consistent; one signature moment.
9. Voice consistent with the visuals: specific, short, tone matched to audience.
10. Fit to audience and category: the same restraint on a kids' app would feel wrong; premium means appropriate.
11. Polish in the details: states, focus rings, empty and error states, alignment, no layout shift.
12. A single signature thing a user could name (Linear speed, Duolingo's tactile buttons, GOV.UK's focus ring).
13. Craft over novelty: Awwwards weights design and usability at 70% and creativity at 20%.

## Where AI output falls short

- Defaults instead of decisions: the same few fonts (Inter everywhere), indigo/purple accents, same hero and card grid.
- No art direction: same layout for a bank and a toy shop; no connection to audience or product world.
- Flat hierarchy: everything gets a card, an icon tile, equal weight, equal shadows.
- Decoration substituted for content: gradients, blur, glow, emoji icons, fake stats and logos, placeholder images.
- Weak type: wrong scale, no display-size tuning, ALL-CAPS eyebrows everywhere, centred body text.
- Colour: too many hues, low-contrast greys, accent used everywhere; dark mode as inverted light.
- Spacing is uniform and cramped or uniformly loose; no rhythm and no clear grouping.
- Motion either absent or applied to everything (fade-up on every section).
- Missing states and edge cases; long text and mobile break.
- Generic copy: "Unlock the power of...", filler headings, names like John Doe.
- No critique loop: output is not looked at, measured or cut back.
- Overcorrection risk: banning a pattern (e.g. cream plus terracotta) then converging on the next default; need a library of directions with reasons, plus a rule to pick for the audience and not for novelty.
